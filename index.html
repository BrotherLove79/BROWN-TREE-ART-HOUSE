Our story

Built by neighbors, for neighbors.

Brown Tree Art House began with a simple belief: every young person deserves space to be seen, safe, and inspired. We create welcoming, culturally grounded environments where creativity becomes a path to confidence, connection, and opportunity.

Through workshops, family events, youth programming, and community partnerships, we support artistic expression and belonging for people of all ages.

What we do

Programs that nurture creativity and community.

🎨

After-School Arts Studio

Hands-on studio sessions with visual arts, creative writing, and collaborative projects led by local teaching artists.

🌱

Youth Leadership & Mentorship

Programs that help young people build confidence, communicate clearly, and lead creative initiatives in their communities.

🤝

Family Arts Nights

Inclusive evenings where families create together, connect with local artists, and build joyful memories.

🎭

Community Events

Exhibitions, performances, workshops, and cultural celebrations designed to bring neighbors together in shared creativity.
